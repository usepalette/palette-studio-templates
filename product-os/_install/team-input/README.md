# Team input

Current product priorities, constraints, and roadmap signals from people involved in the product.
This may be the installer, teammates, or cross-functional contributors.

This is input to product decisions, not the roadmap itself. Initiatives, projects, tasks, status,
and dates remain in the issue tracker when the team uses one.

## Files

- `<name>.md` - the person's current product input. Update in place.
- `roadmap-sync-YYYY-MM-DD.md` - dated recap of a planning or roadmap conversation.

Use `_person-template.md` and `_roadmap-sync-template.md`.
