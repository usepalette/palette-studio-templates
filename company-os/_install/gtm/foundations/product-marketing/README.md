# Product marketing

The bridge between what the product *does* and how the market *hears* it. Load this (with `../brand/`) before writing anything that explains or sells the product.

## What goes here

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `product-overview.md` | What the product is, how it works, who it's for — the plain-language reference. |
| `sales-narrative.md` | The strategic story that makes the product matter — the "why now, why this". |

Positioning and messaging live in `../brand/` (`positioning.md`, `messaging.md`); this folder turns those into the product story sales and marketing actually use.
