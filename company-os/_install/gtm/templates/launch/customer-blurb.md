## How to write this

**What this is:** a short, personal note to a customer who'd care about this — usually a champion who asked for it or hits the problem it solves. Not a broadcast; a message to *one person* (or one account).

**Make it about them, not you.** Lead with the thing they'll care about, ideally something they raised. If you can reference their own words or a past conversation, do — honestly, never faked.

**Keep it short.** 3–5 sentences. One clear ask (try it, hop on a call, share feedback).

**Tone:** how you'd actually message them — warm, direct, no marketing polish. Match the relationship.
