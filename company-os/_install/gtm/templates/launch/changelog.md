## How to write this

**Purpose:** the factual record of what shipped. Not storytelling, not persuasion — clarity. Someone scanning should understand what changed in under 10 seconds.

**Length by tier:**
- Tier 1: 1–3 sentences. Just the facts.
- Tier 2: a short paragraph — what changed + why + what it enables.
- Tier 3: a paragraph + optional bullets of key capabilities. Still concise.

**Lead with what changed, not why.** "You can now filter by team" comes before "We heard from customers that…".

**Concrete language:**
- Good: "You can now…" / "X now includes…"
- Bad: "We're introducing…" / "We're excited to share…" / "A new way to…"

**Link** to docs and/or the blog post if they exist. **Add a visual** (screenshot/GIF) for Tier 2–3 if it helps.

**Tone:** neutral and clear. This is the one place you don't sell — just say what happened.
