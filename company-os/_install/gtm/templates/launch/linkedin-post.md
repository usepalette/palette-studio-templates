## How to write this

**This is not an announcement.** It's a build-in-public post that happens to mention something new. The best ones don't feel like launches — they feel like someone sharing what they noticed, built, or think about a problem.

**Write in the person's voice, not the brand voice.** Read their `../../team/{name}/voice.md` and recent posts first — how they open, paragraph length, whether they use questions.

**Pick who makes sense.** Not everyone posts for every launch — 2–3 people is usually right. Choose by who's closest to the feature and who has a natural angle.

**Each post needs a genuinely different angle.** If you swap the names and they still work, they're too similar.

**Angle options:** the build (what you made and why) · the problem (a real situation) · the customer story (a real interaction) · the opinion (a stance) · the reflection (what you learned).

**What kills it:** "excited to announce," feature lists disguised as insight, corporate third-person, engagement bait ("thoughts?"), humblebrags, anything that sounds machine-written.

**Length:** 100–200 words. Short and sharp beats long and thorough.

**Final test:** would this person actually post this? If they'd rewrite more than 20%, you missed their voice.
