# Launch templates

Fill-in templates for launch content. Used with the workflow in `../../playbooks/launch.md`.

## Before using any template

Load these first — they shape tone, terminology, and positioning across everything you produce:

| File | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| `../../foundations/brand/voice.md` | Tone, language rules, do/don't |
| `../../foundations/brand/messaging.md` | Category, one-liner, key phrases |
| `../../foundations/brand/positioning.md` | How you're different, the frame |

**Terminology matters.** Keep a short list in `messaging.md` of terms to *always use* and terms to *never use* — the words that carry your positioning. Get them wrong and you fight your own story. They apply to *all* external content, not just blog posts.

## Rules that apply to every template

Universal — follow them, don't repeat them in each file:

- **One CTA per piece.** One clear action, not three.
- **No hype language.** No "excited to announce," "game-changing," "revolutionary." See `voice.md`.
- **Problem first, solution second.** Lead with what hurts, then the relief — every format.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Real situations, roles, tools. "Your team lead asks for a status update" beats "streamline alignment."
- **Trace to the messaging brief.** Every piece connects back to the approved `messaging-brief.md`. If you can't connect it, you're drifting.

## Templates

| Template | What | Tiers |
|---|---|---|
| `messaging-brief.md` | Phase 1 output — nail this before anything else | All |
| `changelog.md` | Factual record of what shipped | All |
| `docs-update.md` | Product-docs update guidance | All |
| `community-post.md` | Short post for your community channel | All |
| `blog-post.md` | Announcement post | 2–3 |
| `customer-blurb.md` | Slack/email-ready message for customers | 2–3 |
| `account-messaging.md` | Who cares and why — account matching | 2–3 |
| `linkedin-post.md` | Per-person social posts | 2 (optional), 3 |
| `landing-page.md` | Landing-page content structure | 3 |
| `video-script.md` | Explainer / commercial video script | 3 |

## How to use

1. Follow the workflow in `../../playbooks/launch.md`.
2. Start with `messaging-brief.md` — fill it in during Phase 1 and get it agreed.
3. Once messaging is locked, pick templates by tier (Phase 2).
4. Put the outputs in the launch's folder.
