## How to write this

**What this is:** a short post in your community channel (Slack/Discord). The readers already use the product and are friendly. Don't sell — tell them what shipped and invite a reaction.

**Tone:** channel-native. Like writing in a team channel, not a marketing post. Short paragraphs, line breaks for scanning, casual but not goofy — same plain voice as everything else (`../../foundations/brand/voice.md`), just lighter.

**Length:** ~80–150 words. If it doesn't fit a single message preview, it's too long.

**Structure that works:**
1. One-line headline — what shipped, in their words
2. 3–5 bullets — the most useful bits, not all of them
3. A link to the full changelog or download
4. One sentence inviting reactions or questions

**Avoid:** formal greetings, email-style sign-offs, reusing the changelog copy verbatim, "we're excited to share" / "big news", and engagement bait.

**Emojis:** sparingly — one in the headline if it earns its place, maybe one warm one at the end.
