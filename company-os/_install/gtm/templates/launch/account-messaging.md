## How to write this

**What this is:** the bridge from a launch to your accounts. Instead of blasting everyone, match the launch to the accounts it actually matters to, and give each a tailored angle.

**How to build it:**
1. Scan `../../../accounts/` (briefs + `requests.md`) for who asked for this or hits the problem.
2. For each, note the champion, why it matters *to them*, and the one-line angle.
3. Turn the strongest matches into a `customer-blurb.md` each.

**Be honest about fit.** A weak match with a generic message is worse than not reaching out. Only list accounts where there's a real reason.
