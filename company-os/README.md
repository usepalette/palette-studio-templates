# Company OS

Your whole company as a file system: one folder of markdown for how the company works and what
is active across accounts, GTM, product, engineering, operations, and people.

Built for businesses that want to work in an AI-native way and let different supported agents
work from the same company context.

## What you get

- **Six connected spaces** for accounts, GTM, product, engineering, operations, and people.
  Keep the ones that fit and add the areas your company needs.
- **Works with different AI agents.** `AGENTS.md` holds the shared rules, with small pointers
  that help Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Mistral, and other supported agents use the same
  company context.
- **Four ways to bring in existing context.** Start from Palette's Context Library, documents
  in `_inbox/`, exports from other AI tools, or one short conversation.
- **Lessons and a work log.** `lessons.md` captures what to do differently and `log.md` records
  what happened, so useful context stays available in later sessions.
- **Built-in workflows** add customers, teammates, and new company spaces without hand-copying
  folders.

## How setup works

Company OS starts as a blank folder structure. Say **"set me up"** and choose the easiest source:

- Use connected context such as Palette's Context Library.
- Drop decks, wiki exports, org charts, or other documents into `_inbox/`.
- Use the built-in prompt to bring useful context over from ChatGPT, Claude, Notion, or
  another tool.
- Answer one short round of questions for anything that is not written down yet.

The agent sorts the material, flags what is missing, and confirms the setup before it writes.
Palette Desktop creates a checkpoint first, so you can undo.

## Work from the same company context

Open the folder with Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, or another supported agent. Each one can read
the same company structure, instructions, decisions, and durable lessons.

- "Review this launch using our brand, product, and customer context."
- "What should I know from across the company before working on our hiring plan?"
- "Help me bring the company context I already have in ChatGPT into this workspace."

Everything is plain markdown you can read and edit, with or without Palette.
