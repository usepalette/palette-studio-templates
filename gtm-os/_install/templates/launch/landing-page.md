## How to write this

**Purpose:** one page that makes a visitor understand the thing and want it. It's the messaging brief, expanded and sequenced for someone who arrived cold.

**Sequence the page like an argument:** hook → problem → the shift → what it is → how it works → proof → CTA. Each section earns the scroll to the next.

**Rules:** one primary CTA (repeated, not competing with others). Concrete scenarios over adjectives. Every claim backed by something real. Scannable — headings and subheads carry the story on their own.
