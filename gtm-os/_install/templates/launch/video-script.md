## How to write this

**Purpose:** show the transformation in 30–90 seconds. Video is for *feeling* the before/after, not listing features.

**Open on the problem, not the product.** The first 3 seconds decide whether they keep watching — start in a moment of pain or recognition, not a logo.

**One idea.** A launch video makes one point land. If you're explaining three things, it's three videos.

**Write for the ear and eye together.** Voiceover (VO) is what they hear; on-screen is what they see. They should reinforce, not repeat. Keep VO short — people read faster than you talk.

**End on the one thing + a single CTA.**
