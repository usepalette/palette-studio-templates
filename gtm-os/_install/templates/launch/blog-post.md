## How to write this

**Length:** Tier 2: 300–500 words. Tier 3: 500–800. If you're over, cut — don't summarize.

**Opening:** never start with "We're excited to announce" or "Today we're launching." Open with the problem or an observation the reader recognizes. Create tension or recognition in the first two sentences.

**Tone:** like explaining it to a smart colleague over coffee. Concrete, not abstract — "When your lead asks for a status update and you spend 20 minutes gathering it from three tools" beats "streamline your workflow."

**Structure:** problem → what you built → how it works → what changes. Feel the pain, see the solution, know what to do next. Don't meander.

**Avoid:** feature soup (capabilities instead of outcomes), hedging ("helps you," "enables teams to"), and explaining what they already know.

**Self-check:** read it out loud. If you'd roll your eyes hearing it, rewrite it.
