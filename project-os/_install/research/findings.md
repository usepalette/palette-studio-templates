## Top themes

- **Checkout feels risky on mobile** — in discovery interviews, customers hesitated at payment because the form was long and the buttons were small. Cited by most participants.
- **"I just want to check my order"** — the most common support question is order status, which the portal doesn't surface well.

## Surprises

- Customers didn't care about the visual redesign nearly as much as about speed — "if it loaded fast I wouldn't mind how it looks."

## Open questions
