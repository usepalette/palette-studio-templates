# Project brief

The single page a new joiner reads first. Keep it tight — link out to the detail rather than duplicating it.

> **Sample brief** — this describes a fictional project ("Acme Co. — Customer Portal Rebuild") so you can see the shape. Replace it with your real project (`/onboard` does this).

## Background

Acme Co. sells to small businesses through an online customer portal. The current portal is several years old, slow on mobile, and expensive to change. Acme brought us in to rebuild it and lift conversion.

## The problem

- The portal is slow, especially on mobile — where most customers are.
- Checkout loses a lot of customers partway through.
- The support team answers the same handful of questions all day because customers can't self-serve.

## Audience

- **Customers** — small-business buyers using the portal, mostly on mobile.
- **Acme support** — the team fielding questions today.
- **Acme product** — the sponsor who owns the outcome.

## What success looks like

- Checkout completion up from 62% to 80%+.
- 40% of common support questions handled by self-serve help.
- Mobile pages loading in under 2 seconds. See [`goals.md`](./goals.md).

## Constraints

- **Timeline:** discovery + build to a September go-live. See [`../admin/timeline.md`](../admin/timeline.md).
- **Technical:** keep the current payment provider; migrate existing customer data.
- **Organizational:** Acme's support lead (Priya) is the domain expert; a security review is required before launch.

## Out of scope

See [`../admin/scope.md`](../admin/scope.md) for the maintained list. Headline: this is the customer-facing portal only — internal admin tools are untouched.
